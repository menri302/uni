# What is Finance?

> [!abstract] Overview
> **Finance** (Finanzwirtschaft) comprises **all financial measures** for **planning, managing and controlling cash flows** in a firm.
> Source: Gabler Wirtschaftslexikon

## The Objectives of Finance

| Objective                      | What it asks                         | What it means               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| **Financing** *(Finanzierung)* | How does a firm **get** money?       | **Raising** funds in a firm |
| **Investment** *(Investition)* | How does a firm **spend** its money? | **Using** funds in a firm   |

---

# Financing Options

|                                                                                           | **Financing by Owners** *(Beteiligungsfinanzierung)*       | **Financing by Creditors** *(Fremdfinanzierung)*                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **External Financing** *(Außenfinanzierung)*<br>Raising new capital on the capital market | **Equity financing**<br>Examples: partner deposits, stocks | **Debt financing**<br>Examples: bank loans, bonds                    |
| **Internal Financing** *(Innenfinanzierung)*<br>Using capital that is already in the firm | **Self-financing** *(Selbstfinanzierung)*                  | **Financing using provisions** *(Finanzierung durch Rückstellungen)* |

## Special Cases

- **External financing:** ==**Mezzanine capital**==, e.g. convertible bonds (*Wandelanleihen*)
- **Internal financing:** **Financing through depreciation** (*Finanzierung aus Abschreibungen*) offsets
